XBOX INFO

Some information on Xbox

Xbox is a gaming company owned by Microsoft, known for their Xbox consoles, their great value Game Pass, and games like Halo and forza.

Xbox was originally created in 2001 by Microsoft, of the the worlds largest tech companies. In 2005 the 2nd Xbox was released, the Xbox 360, which was the best selling xbox console to date (84M sold). Over the years Xbox has released a variety of different consoles and games, leading them to be a successful gaming company.
